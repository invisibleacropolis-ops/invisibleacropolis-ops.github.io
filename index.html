Invisible Acropolis

This is not a homepage. It is a valley. Every monument of light on the horizon is a doorway into a different experiment — fly toward one and step through.

  • Fly

    Move the mouse to look. Hold W to surge forward, S to drift, A/D to strafe, Space/Shift to climb or dive.

  • Approach

    Follow the compass at the top of your view — every pip is a doorway. Near a monument, its waypoint card rises from the bottom.

  • Step through

    Press E — or click the card — to travel to that page. Esc frees the cursor.
